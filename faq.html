FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Find answers to common questions about our services, packages and consultation process.

Who are your services for?

Our services are designed for small businesses in Saskatchewan that need practical support with billing, routine transactions and back-office administration.

What is included in the Essentials package?

Essentials includes up to 50 routine transactions per month, up to 10 customer invoices, light invoice-status tracking, basic quality checks and a monthly summary.

What is the difference between Essentials and Essentials Plus?

Essentials Plus includes everything in Essentials, plus straightforward bank transaction matching and reconciliation of one agreed business bank account per month.

What happens if I need more than the monthly limits?

Additional work can be discussed separately. We will confirm the scope and any additional cost with you before starting the extra work.

Do you provide tax or legal advice?

No. Our services focus on routine administrative, billing and transaction support. Tax, legal and other professional advisory services are outside the standard packages.

Can I purchase a package directly through the website?

During the initial launch, we use a consultation process rather than direct online purchasing. This allows us to understand your business needs and confirm the appropriate service scope.

How do I get started?

Simply submit a consultation request through our Contact page. We will review your request and discuss the next steps with you.

Will online payments be available later?

Online recurring payments may be added in the future through a secure payment provider such as Stripe or Square.

Still have a question?

Contact us and we'll be happy to discuss your business requirements.
