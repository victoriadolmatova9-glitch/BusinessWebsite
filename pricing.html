Understanding What's Included

Our packages are designed for routine, clearly defined administrative support.

Included in the packages

  • Routine monthly transaction processing
  • Customer invoice preparation within the agreed limit
  • Basic administrative checks
  • Monthly activity reporting

Excluded unless separately agreed

  • Transactions exceeding monthly limits
  • Additional bank accounts
  • Historical bookkeeping cleanup
  • Payroll processing
  • Tax preparation and filing
  • Legal, tax or financial advice
  • Complex accounting services

Additional work can be discussed and quoted separately before any work begins. Exact service scope will be confirmed during the initial consultation.
